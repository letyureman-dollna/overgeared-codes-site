How to Get Ember Stones in Overgeared (Roblox)

Updated: August 24, 2026 · BETA material

Ember Stones are an upgrade material in Overgeared [BETA], confirmed as part of the UPDATE3 code rewards. They fuel weapon upgrade paths — the resource that turns a good sword into a boss-clearing one.

Related materials in the same economy: Xeros Tears (100 from UPDATE1, 50 from UPDATE3), Crystal Storm Stones (30 from UPDATE3) and Xerath's Gems (50 from UPDATE1).

What Ember Stones Are Used For

  • Weapon upgrades — high-tier upgrade paths consume Ember Stones alongside Gold and Gems.
  • Boss-tier weapons — the biggest sinks pair boss drops with material payouts; bank stones until you have the weapon worth spending on (see Best Swords).

Don't Waste Them Early

With only 50 available from codes right now, the optimal play is: claim UPDATE3, don't upgrade your starter sword past the early tiers, and spend the stones on your first boss-tier weapon. One maxed weapon clears more content than three half-upgraded ones.
